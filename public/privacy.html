Privacy

Lekh Patro · last updated 15 August 2026

Lekh Patro has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It sets no cookies. Nothing you write is stored on a server, because there is no server that belongs to Lekh Patro — the site is static files and everything else happens in your browser.

That said, four things do cause a request to leave your device, and this page names all four rather than rounding down to “nothing is sent anywhere”.

What stays on your device

None of the following ever leaves the device, in the browser or in the Android app:

  • Everything you type, and the Roman → Devanagari transliteration of it.
  • The cheat sheet and the transliteration dictionary — both ship with the app.
  • Reading text out of images and PDFs. The OCR engine runs inside your browser; the file is never uploaded.
  • The Nepali calendar itself. The Bikram Sambat grid is computed locally and the bundled festival table is part of the download.
  • On-device translation, once you have chosen to download the model. It then runs offline.

What leaves your device

1. Online translation — this one includes your text

If you use the Translate tab with the online engine, the text you are translating is sent to a third-party translation service to be translated. Their handling of it is governed by their policies, not this one.

translate.googleapis.com · api.mymemory.translated.net

This is the only feature that transmits anything you have written. Choosing the on-device engine avoids it entirely.

2. Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so opening Lekh Patro makes a request that reveals your IP address and browser to Google, as almost any website does. No content of yours is involved.

fonts.googleapis.com · fonts.gstatic.com

3. Calendar updates

The Patro tab refreshes its festival and holiday list from a public open-source calendar dataset hosted on GitHub. It is a plain file download — the request says which month is being viewed and nothing about you beyond your IP address.

raw.githubusercontent.com

4. The offline translation model, if you ask for it

Downloading the on-device translation model fetches it from Hugging Face. This only ever happens after you explicitly confirm the download, and never on its own.

huggingface.co

What is stored on your device

All of this is ordinary browser storage on your own machine. Clearing site data removes it, and none of it is readable by anyone else.

  • Your theme choice and which tab you were on.
  • Whether you have confirmed and downloaded the offline translation model.
  • A cache of the app itself, so it works offline, and — if you downloaded it — the translation model.

The Android app and widget

The Android app is the same web app in a wrapper, so everything above applies to it unchanged. The home-screen widget is separate and simpler: it reads only the calendar data bundled inside the app and makes no network requests at all.

Children

Lekh Patro is a general-purpose typing and calendar tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect personal information.

Changes

If what the app sends ever changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.
